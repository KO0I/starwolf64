# Andrew with Fox's body

Andrew's revised split-color head is attached to the original Fox title body from the reviewed character archive. The body keeps Fox's exact geometry, normals, UVs, limb transforms, idle animation and running animation. Its textures are recolored to match the supplied sheet: purple jacket and outer trousers, black undersuit and gloves, red scarf, gray cuffs and boots, silver belt. Fox's shirt name badge is removed.

The GLB uses meters, +Y up and +Z forward, with Idle, Run, and Talk clips. It uses the original rigid limb hierarchy rather than a skinned replacement rig. The head was revised after comparing the native Andross radio and ending portraits. A wider simian muzzle, paired nostrils, heavier brow ridge and broader jaw suggest the family relationship. Andrew keeps the sheet's pointed ears, split face colors and narrow ivory eyes. head-before-andross-review.glb preserves the previous head. head-review.json records the design choices and source hashes. The head is reduced to 160 triangles, with the previous 246-triangle head retained as head-246.glb. The head is 20% taller than the previous 160-triangle version, anchored at the neck; width and depth are unchanged. Its facial features, ears and jaw share the vertical extension, and the talking jaw pivot follows the new proportions. The body retains its previous size. head-160-before-height.glb and andrew-before-head-height.glb retain the earlier proportions. Eyes, nose, nostrils and ear insets keep their topology; the skull, jaw, hidden brow and ear surfaces, and mouth are simplified. head-reduction.json records the per-part triangle budgets. The editable Blender file and static OBJ are imported from the delivered GLB. All recolored textures are embedded in the GLB. The OBJ textures are included alongside it.

Run `python3 -B tools/andrew_character.py` in the repository to rebuild. Run `python3 -B tools/andrew_character.py --check` to verify the delivered geometry against Fox's source body. Serve this folder with `python3 -m http.server 8653` and open `http://localhost:8653/viewer.html` for the viewer. The viewer uses pinned Three.js dependencies from jsDelivr.

Retain CREDITS.md and README_TheRegressor.txt when distributing. Fox and underlying Star Fox designs belong to Nintendo. The head was constructed from the user's supplied references. This remains a standalone asset without a game runtime assignment.
