# Krystal 64 credits

The Regressor modeled and rigged **Krystal 64**. King-Jion exported and converted the model. Alain Jullien created the concept art. The supplied credits also name Project Spearhead, Wreckingcanon, and Mr. Krystal for funding and finishing. Rareware created Krystal. Nintendo holds the character copyright.

This pack adapts the hair, jewelry and tail meshes and eye texture artwork to the supplied Fox limb rig. The eye artwork is cut from the original `Krystal_TEXT.png`, masked around the eyelids and mirrored for the opposite side. The lower-detail eye cards are fitted to the Fox head's original sockets, and its baked eye textures are cleared. The fit, reduced on-foot geometry, texture region extraction, jumpsuit and integration are changes made for this pack. The original README is included verbatim at `sources/krystal_adaptation/README.txt`.

Krystal and Kami now share the OEM-derived Fox body proportions and rigid limb rig. Kami retains her chameleon head and spiral tail with all headset hardware removed. Krystal's outfit uses The Regressor design as a clothing reference, including a purple jumpsuit, fur-blue scarf, silver gauntlets/boots, and an arm-marking excerpt from the original texture. The vest is omitted. Outfit styling changes UVs and materials; both characters also have 20% narrower and shallower thigh segments, with lengths and pivots preserved.

[The Regressor](https://sketchfab.com/the-regressor) · [King-Jion](https://sketchfab.com/king-jion) · [Alain Jullien](https://www.artstation.com/alain_jullien) · [Krystal Archive](https://krystalarchive.com/)

## Vitzy

Vitzy is an original character by [VitzyPie](https://x.com/VitzyPie). The fan-made pilot head and six-joint rig follow the two supplied face references. Bill has a Wolfen-style cockpit cutaway with that head mounted inside. No Vitzy body mesh is included. Wolfen is a Star Fox design from Nintendo. Original radio portraits remain available.

## Original pilot lineup

Fox McCloud, Falco Lombardi, Peppy Hare, Slippy Toad, and Wolf O'Donnell use the supplied Star Fox 64 model pack, including its original textures and animation clips. Star Fox characters and original game assets are Nintendo's. Wolf's supplied appearance uses the project's Wolf mod on the Fox limb rig.

Wolf's raised collar is adapted from Falco's original eight-triangle title-model collar, fitted below Wolf's jaw. Its outer shell uses a purple leather grain texture; the inner lining uses a white wool texture. The full Wolf character is scaled by 1.10. The separate cockpit-head download retains its original scale.

Krystal's body grows by 1.08 in height and 1.04 in width and depth. Her restored face and previous complete-head adjustment are retained, with the head's dimensions preserved as the body grows. The GLB downloads, rendered turntables, and animation previews use the same saved model data.
