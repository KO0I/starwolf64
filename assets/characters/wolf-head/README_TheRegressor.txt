Krystal 64
==========

Krystal, as she might look if she had appeared in Star Fox 64. This low-poly rig was created by The Regressor.



Features
========

* Blaster prop
* Original Maya rig in Maya Binary format (.mb)
* Numerous exported versions: COLLADA (.dae), Autodesk FBX (.fbx), and Wavefront (.obj, no rigging)
* Accessible Blender version (.blend)
