Every portrait in the ROM

Star Fox 64, US v1.1. All 76 radio/menu character slots and 3 static slots, including 11 unused slots. Below those are 16 additional map portraits, portrait components, assembled frames and character illustrations. The radio/menu portraits contain 60 distinct character frames. Duplicate copies remain visible.

The unused Katina pair shows an alternate Bill portrait. Other unused character slots duplicate frames used in other asset banks. Red radio windows reuse the same portrait pixels. Expert Fox and all Venom 2 Star Wolf variants are used.
