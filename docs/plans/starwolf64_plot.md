# Star Wolf 64 Plot (reverse Star Fox 64)

**Premise** – The battle starts on the hostile world **Venom**, where the Star Wolf fleet has been driven out after a catastrophic defeat. The team must fight back across the Lylat system, reclaim lost allies, and ultimately retake **Corneria** from the tyrant **General Pigma**.

### Plot Beats

1. **Venom – The Fall**
   - *Wolf* pilots the battered Arwing, barely escaping a massive Pigma‑forced orbital bombardment.
   - *Kami* patches the damaged navigation array, buying time for a desperate warp‑jump.
   - *Krystal* uses her telepathic link to locate hidden rebel survivors.
   - *Panther* leads a ground assault to sabotage the enemy’s power core, buying the fleet a narrow window to flee.

2. **Space‑Lane 1 – Re‑gathering**
   - The fleet regroups at **Sector 7**; Wolf discovers a hidden cache of experimental **Flux‑Boosters**.
   - Kami calibrates the boosters for a high‑speed jump to the next sector.
   - Krystal decodes a corrupted transmission revealing Pigma’s plan: a coordinated strike on **Corneria** using a fleet of **Puma‑Drones**.

3. **Sector 2 – The Lost Outpost**
   - Panther raids a Pigma‑controlled outpost to rescue captured **Cornerian** engineers.
   - The engineers provide schematics for a **Resonance Shield** that can protect Corneria’s defenses.

4. **Sector 3 – Ambush at Nebula X**
   - Wolf and Kami engage a swarm of Puma‑Drones; the Flux‑Boosters give them a temporary edge.
   - Krystal’s telepathy warns of a hidden minefield; the team navigates safely, turning the ambush into a trap that destroys several enemy ships.

5. **Corneria – Final Assault**
   - Using the Resonance Shield, the fleet blocks Pigma’s barrage.
   - Wolf flies a direct strike on Pigma’s command ship, disabling its core.
   - Kami and Panther coordinate a ground assault, capturing the General’s headquarters.
   - Krystal confronts Pigma in a psychic duel, breaking his resolve and forcing his surrender.

6. **Victory & Restoration**
   - Corneria’s citizens celebrate. The rescued engineers begin rebuilding the planet’s orbital defenses.
   - Star Wolf vows to keep the Lylat system free from tyranny, with the team now united stronger than before.

### Sample Dialogue

- **Wolf (over comms):** “We’re not retreating—we’re turning the tide. Hold the line, fire those boosters!”
- **Kami (in the cockpit):** “Flux‑Boosters calibrated. One jump, and we’re out of their sight.”
- **Krystal (to the rebels):** “Your minds are safe with us. We’ll bring Corneria back before they can finish the siege.”
- **Panther (on the ground):** “Power core’s down! Move! We’ve got minutes before the whole sector collapses.”
- **General Pigma (captured):** “You think a few rebels can stop the Lylat Empire?”
- **Krystal (psychic link):** “Your empire is built on fear. Fear ends when you’re no longer in command.”
